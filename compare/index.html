Why a native harness matters

An agent harness runs all day. A native one starts instantly, idles at near-zero CPU, stays under 60 MB of RAM (in our tests OpenCode's terminal UI used 383 MB and Cursor about 1.6 GB) and feels like part of your OS. You can leave it open beside your IDE, your browser and your build without noticing it.
